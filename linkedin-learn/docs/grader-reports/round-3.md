A clean, modern, consistent system — spacing, type scale, cards, hero gradient, ring, path cards, filled topic bars — and **both themes are intentional**, not a toggle (`probe-library-dark.png`, `probe-dashboard-dark.png`): dark tags stay legible, "Reading" amber reads clearly, pressed chips carry a solid fill. Residual contrast nits (why not 9), by impact:
1. **The pressed AI & Design topic chip is sub-AA.** `js/app.js:120` gives a *selected* topic chip `background:<raw topic color>;color:#15171f`, using the **raw** color (not the 86%+#fff lightening the tags got). Dark ink #15171f on raw #8B5CF6 = **4.22:1** — below the 4.5:1 floor. The other seven topic chips pass (5.07–8.33:1). Visible as the active "AI & Design" pill in `probe-library-chips-light.png`. Fix: lighten the selected-chip fill the same way tags do (`color-mix(in srgb, t.color 86%, #fff)`), or darken the `ai-design` topic color toward ~#7c4ddb.
2. **The "STARTER" badge label is sub-AA.** `css/style.css:146-148` — `--badge-ink` #0b6e62 on the light teal mix = **3.40:1** for 11 px bold (up from round 2's 2.20). Decorative, informational-only, and hidden < 560 px, so low impact. Fix: deepen the ink (e.g. #0a5a50) or raise the font size/weight to the large-text threshold.

Residual (minor, why not 10):
1. **The number-key 1–4 quiz shortcut is inert until focus enters the quiz.** `js/app.js:351` attaches the `keydown` handler to `.quiz`, but after each `draw()`/route the focused element is `#view` — an *ancestor* of `.quiz` — so the keystroke never bubbles to the handler; pressing "3" on a fresh question marks nothing (probe A), while pressing "3" after tabbing onto an option works (probe B marks the correct option). A bonus per the rubric, so low impact. Fix: bind the handler to `document`/`#view`, or focus the first option (or give `.quiz` `tabindex="-1"` and focus it) in `draw()`.
